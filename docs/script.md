# EN
Aloha! My name is Tsumugi. I'm 12 years old. I love dance of hula.
I have been doing hula for 9 years.
Today, I would like to tell you why I love hula.  
First, do you know about hula?
Hula is a traditional Hawaiian dance.
It started as a dance to honor the Hawaiian gods.
In hula, we use our hands to tell a story.
We show feelings, nature, and love through our movements.
For example, tears, waves, rain, and wind.
It's also important to dance with a natural smile.
I want people to smile and become interested in hula when they watch my hula.  
Another important part of hula is the spirit of "ALOHA”.
"ALOHA is not only a greeting. It means kindness, caring, and living in harmony with others.
I think the best thing about hula is that the ALOHA spirit helps us love ourselves and care for other people. It can make everyone feel happy.  
Hula is very special to me.
It has helped me become more confident, I have also become used to being on stage.
I think this will help me in the future, so I want to keep practicing and become a better dancer.  
I hope you learn something about hula today.
Hula always helps me stay positive.
I will be very happy if you are more interested in hula after listening to my speech!
I hope you will try hula someday!
It is fun!  
Thank you for listening! Aloha!

# JP

まず、皆さんはフラを知っていますか?

フラでは、感情、自然、愛などの歌詞のストーリーを手の動きで表します。
例えば、涙や波、雨、風などです。 (身振り手振りを入れる)

私は、「ALOHA」の精神を一人一人が持て、自分のことも、みんなのことも愛せ、ハッピーにできるところがフラの魅力だと思います。
